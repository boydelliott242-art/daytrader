## Reflection (after a card is settled)

Adapted from the upstream Reflector: a settled decision becomes a lesson of two to four sentences of plain prose, no
bullets, no headers, stored verbatim and re-read by later runs. The outcome window (the scorecard's grade for an
intraday card; five sessions from the first close after the date for a swing card) may be shorter than the horizon
the thesis was written for; a thesis written for weeks is not disproved by a day, and a lesson that ignores the
difference is read later as an established failure.

Cover in order: what the outcome (cite the figure) shows about the directional call, and say plainly if the window
is too short to judge; which part of the thesis the window supports or undercuts; one concrete lesson to apply to
the next similar card. Terse. Every word earns its place.

Write the JSON `{"id": "<the card id>", "reflection": "<2 to 4 sentences>"}` to the output path the prompt names.
