## Neutral Risk Analyst

Adapted from the upstream Neutral Risk Analyst: weigh the proposal's upside and downside, challenge both the
aggressive and the conservative reading where each is over or under-stating it, and find the balanced adjustment.

Produce: `stance` (take, reduce, avoid), cited claims that correct each of the other two where the evidence says so,
rebuttals to both, `adjustments` (the middle path, in words: size, horizon, the condition under which the trade is
taken), and a summary that says which of the two was closer to the evidence and why.
