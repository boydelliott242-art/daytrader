Fine print

the upstream README's words, unchanged

Not financial advice. This is software that reads public data and does arithmetic. It does not know your situation and it cannot see the future. Most people who day trade lose money. You can lose money. Decide for yourself, and talk to a licensed professional if you need advice.

No trade goes through until you say yes. Only /trader-broker can lead to an order, and only after /trader-risk passes that exact order and you type yes for it in the chat. Set your brokerage tool to "Needs approval" in Claude (Customize, Connectors, tool permissions), never "Always allow", so Claude's own "Allow once / Deny" card is a second check. No options, margin, short selling or leverage unless you turn them on.

Paper first. "paper": true is the default in config.example.json, and Alpaca's MCP server defaults to its paper account. Keys live in environment variables, never in the chat.

The Time Machine is a simulation, not a prediction. It is built from the stock's own last 60 days of 5-minute bars. The future can do things those 60 days never did: an earnings report, a halt, a headline. Treat its odds as a reality check, never as a promise.

A stop is a plan, not a guarantee. In a fast market or across a gap, a stop order can fill well past its price. The max loss shown is the loss at the stop price.

The data is free and unofficial. Yahoo Finance's chart endpoint is not an official API: it can lag, change or rate-limit you. The scanner and the entry timer read 1-minute or 5-minute bars, not every tick. Check anything that matters against your broker before you act on it.

The rest of the upstream fine print

Day-trading rules. FINRA removed the $25,000 pattern-day-trader minimum on June 4, 2026 (Regulatory Notice 26-10), and brokers may phase the change in until October 20, 2027, so yours may still apply the old rule. If it does, set "pdt_guard": true and /trader-risk counts your day trades.

Congress's trades are public, not inside information. Members must disclose trades over $1,000 within 45 days (the STOCK Act). The Pelosi skill reads those public filings the minute they post.

Taxes. Day-trading gains are short-term gains, and frequent trading makes wash sales likely. Nothing here is tax advice.
